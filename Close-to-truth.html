Close to truth

By Nidhi Chandra

So close but far apart
Where heavens lie
Where worlds meet
I kneel down at truths
And forget all lies
I close my eyes
To see emptiness
To look at dark
The dark colored
And colors were blank
I set a life
To see its endless
To point at myself
The question ends
And truths unfold
So close was I
To halt at every step
To close my eyes
And blankness fades
The truth fades
And beauty glows
Amongst the endless.
