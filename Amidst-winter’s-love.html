Amidst winter’s love

By Nidhi Chandra

Amidst the glow of winter
When monsoon ends
There’s a spark
Of glowing beauty
The sun blurs
The cloud whispers
Amidst the walking winds
River sparkles up
The sea shore splits up
Into divine parts
And then joins again
The cool breeze blows up
And the winter sparkles
The blue sky brightens
This is the call of love
The winter’s love
The love of falling
The love of rejoining
The love of seeking
And amidst these wideness
The glow of truth shines.
