The Tragedy

By Nidhi Chandra

I found options
And I made a choice
Some enchanted thoughts
Some falling emotions
Some fading memories
Some provoked images
Some lifeless words
They were mine
As if truths hide
The life trembled
In front of me
I carried disasters
Rain and storms
And tragedy laughed
Harder, hearing my misery
All I grasped was a hint
Of its dutiful plans
Was it destiny?
Or just insanity
Or maybe innocence
I accepted the truth
And denied the reality
All I found was hidden misery
And unaccepted truths.
