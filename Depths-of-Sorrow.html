The Depths of sorrow

By Nidhi Chandra

The depths of sorrow
The wild corners
I heard from the depths
The whispers were unnamed
The corners were separated
Like worlds that never met
Treasures did get locked
And keys lost
Somewhere beneath my memories
Stars hide at daylight
Behind an unfamiliar sky
And amidst the glow of sunshine
I saw gathered clouds
Equally partitioned by silver lining
And within me the storms were carried
The peace departed
I encountered a sorrow
The truth hid itself
The heavens waited
For me to surrender a part
My dignity was uphill
Standing beyond my reach
All I could hear was nostalgia
For the worlds were separated
And I was flattered.
